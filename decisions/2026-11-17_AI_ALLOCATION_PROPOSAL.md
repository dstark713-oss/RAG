# Fundamental second pass and AI allocation proposal — 2026-11-17

As-of: 2026-11-17. This is a research proposal, not an executed order. It is an independent discretionary overlay for discussion; it does not modify model1.

## Decision in one paragraph
The original model-selected basket (MGKL, MSNG, BSPB, ASTR) is not acceptable unchanged. MGKL is a hard veto due to missed bond coupon payments and a full default. MSNG has deteriorating operating profitability despite revenue growth. BSPB is cheap but its 9M26 earnings and net interest income are under pressure, with higher provisioning. ASTR is the only one of the four selected names I would retain, but at a restrained weight pending 9M results/cash-conversion review. I would promote T, X5 and GCHE from the model's wider 15-name candidate list based on stronger current fundamental evidence and valuation snapshots, while controlling position sizes for credit, leverage and liquidity risks.

## Why the original selected names change
### MGKL — VETO, 0%
Reported full default on coupon payment of RUB 24.66m for 001P-07 on 16 Nov after prior technical default; additional technical defaults on other issues and rating downgrade to C|ru|. A sixth technical default was reported 17 Nov. This is a credit event, not ordinary equity volatility. Do not allocate pending a credible restructuring and verified restoration of payment capacity.
Sources: https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/9110746 ; https://www.vedomosti.ru/investments/news/2026/11/17/1386666-mgkl-tehdefolt

### MSNG — MONITOR / 0%
Official 9M26 IFRS: revenue +16.5% to RUB 225.224bn, EBITDA -21.4% to RUB 16.642bn, operating profit -54.5% to RUB 3.253bn, net profit -48.3% to RUB 6.179bn. Costs grew faster than revenue; fuel costs were a key factor. This is a negative earnings-quality trend. Not a credit veto, but not attractive enough to retain in the proposed portfolio.
Sources: https://www.mosenergo.ru/press/news/2026/11/813/ ; https://www.mosenergo.ru/press/news/2026/11/812/

September improved (monthly net profit +60.7% y/y), so the trend may be stabilizing, but the 9M picture is still weaker. Valuation snapshot around P/E 3.7, P/B 0.51 is cheap, but low multiples do not compensate automatically for falling earnings and provisioning.

## Method and limitations
- This allocation is a discretionary research proposal, not a replacement of model1's signal logic.
- Model score is not probability of profit.
- Price/multiple snapshots are third-party and may have different update times/accounting periods. Treat them as rough valuation checks, not exact intrinsic value estimates.
- For 17 Nov historical decisions, verify each publication timestamp relative to the signal-generation timestamp. In particular, Cherkizovo's 9M results appeared on 17 Nov; if the release became public after the model signal, it is not eligible for that signal-time decision.
- No full 15-name standardized PIT financial model or complete 96-name correlation replay was performed here.
- Next required research: retrieve ASTR 9M report when published; OZPH 9M on 25 Nov; T's 3Q detailed IFRS statements and credit quality; X5 FCF/capex and dividend cash cover; GCHE liquidity/free-float and debt schedule; bank/insurer sector-specific data; exact signal timestamp and next-open execution prices.

## Review triggers
- T: asset quality, cost of risk, capital adequacy, dilution/transaction terms.
- X5: margin trend, capex, FCF after leases, debt cost.
- ASTR: 9M shipments, receivables, cash conversion, capitalized development costs.
- GCHE: interest coverage, net debt, free float and turnover.
- Any deterioration in credit/liquidity or corporate governance can reduce weights to zero. No automatic stop/target or execution price is specified by this fundamental note.
